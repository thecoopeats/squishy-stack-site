Squishy Club Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

The short version

  • Squishy Club does not collect, store or share personal information.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, no chat, and no contact between players.
  • Game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The one purchase (Deluxe) is handled by Apple or Google and sits behind a parental gate.

Who we are

Squishy Club is a game for children ages 4 to 8, made by Juiced Tech ("we"). Questions about this policy: juiced34tech@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, photos, contacts, location, device advertising identifiers or any other personal information, and it has no sign-in.

Information stored on the device

To remember progress, the game saves a small file on the device: the squishies collected, Fluff, Garden stars, the box timer, and the sound and music settings. It never leaves the device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it, and grown-ups can also erase it from the game's grown-ups area.

Data retention

We keep no children's personal information, so we have nothing to retain or delete on our side. The progress file stays on the device only until the app is deleted or a grown-up erases it in the grown-ups area.

Purchases

Squishy Club is free. Grown-ups can buy one optional upgrade, Deluxe, from the grown-ups area, which is protected by a parental gate. The purchase is made through the Apple App Store or Google Play under the account holder's own terms and privacy policy. We never see payment details; the store only tells the game that Deluxe was bought, so it can be unlocked and restored later.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The game shows no advertising and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. It does not share or sell any information. The only outside service it talks to is the device's app store, for the Deluxe purchase.

Links

The only link in the game is to this policy, and it sits behind the parental gate.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to review, change or delete on our side. We designed the game to follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR's rules for children, and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and to meet the Apple Kids Category and Google Play Families requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. We won't start collecting personal information without updating this policy and getting any consent the law requires first.
